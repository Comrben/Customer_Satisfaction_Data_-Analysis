Customer Satisfaction Analytics Project | Excel Dashboard | Pivot Table | 10K Customer Analyzed
An end-to-end data analytics  project analyzing satisfaction of 10,000 customers across Lagos, Abuja, Benin etc
1.Project Overview
This project analyzes customer satisfaction data to understand customer behaviour, loyalty and geographical distriburion. The goal is to move average satisfaction from 3.0 to 4.0+
2.Business Problem
Company has 10,000 but satisfaction is stuck at neutral 3.0/5. High acquisition, low loyalty. Management needs to know why and where to improve.
3.Objectives
Calculate KPIs (Total Customers, Average Satisfaction) 
Analyze satisfaction by city and segment
Identify insights and give actionable recommendations


4.Dataset Overview
10,000 rows containing: customer ID, (Lagos, Abuja, Benin etc), Customer segment (Corperate, Loyal, New, Returning), Satisfaction score (1-5).
5.Tools & Techniques used
Microsoft Excel: Data cleaning, Pivot Tables, COUNT vs SUM vs AVERAGE, Bar/Area/Donut Charts, Dasshboard Design
6.Data Cleaning & Transformation
Removed duplicates and blank customer IDs
Fixed N/A in segment column 
Changed customer ID aggregate from SUM to COUNT (Key learning)
Standardized City namers


7.EDA
Checked distribution of scores, segment performance and city concentration before visualyzing.
8.KPIs
Total Customers: 10,000
Average Satisfaction: 3.0/5.0


10.Key Insights (5)
1.Lagos  & Abuja contribute >60% of Customers - over dependence risk.
2.Average score 3.0 = neutral zone - customers can easily leave
3.Corporate or loyal score high (4.0+), New customer score low (2.5) onboarding issue.
4.Many customers rated 1-2 - urgent service failure
5.Acquisition is strong but Retention to Loyal is weak.


11.Recommendations
1.Expand in Benin to reducecity risk
2.Call all 1-2 rated customers for recovery
3.Create New customer onboarding program.
4.Lunch Royalty Rewards to convert Returning to Loya
5.Track Satisfaction Monthly.
